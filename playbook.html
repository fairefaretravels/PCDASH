# First 5 Jobs Playbook ### Post-Construction Final Cleaning — Saneca --- ## Overview This playbook takes you from zero to a repeatable, referral-generating post-construction cleaning operation over your first five jobs. Each job has a specific strategic purpose — you're not just cleaning, you're building proof, relationships, systems, and pricing confidence in that order. | Job | Purpose | |---|---| | 1 | The Proof Builder | | 2 | The Referral Builder | | 3 | The System Test | | 4 | The Repeat Customer | | 5 | Raise Your Standard | --- ## Job #1: The Proof Builder **Goal:** Get your first completed project, photos, and testimonial. **Do:** - Be extremely detailed - Take before/after photos - Use your checklist - Communicate professionally **Do not:** - Work for free unless there is a strategic reason - Promise unlimited cleaning **Pricing:** Aim for a fair introductory rate, not a giveaway. > Example — Small final clean: **$500–$1,000** --- ## Job #2: The Referral Builder **Goal:** Create a relationship. **At completion, ask:** > "We appreciate the opportunity to help with this project. Do you have any upcoming renovations or builds where we can support your team?" **Get:** - Contractor name - Project manager contact - Permission to use photos --- ## Job #3: The System Test Treat it like you have employees. Use every piece of your process — this job is testing the system, not just the clean. - ☐ Estimate - ☐ Scope of work - ☐ Schedule - ☐ Cleaning checklist - ☐ Final inspection - ☐ Invoice --- ## Job #4: The Repeat Customer **Your goal:** Turn *"one job"* into *"every time we finish a project, call Saneca."* **Ask:** > "Would it be helpful if we stayed on your vendor list for future final cleans?" --- ## Job #5: Raise Your Standard By job five, you should have: - ✅ Photos - ✅ Reviews - ✅ Contractor references - ✅ Pricing confidence - ✅ Better estimating skills Now you stop chasing random jobs. You start targeting bigger contracts. --- ## Pricing Your First Jobs **Formula:** ``` Labor Hours × Hourly Rate + Supplies + Travel + Profit ``` **Example — Two cleaners, 8 hours each:** | Line Item | Calculation | Amount | |---|---|---| | Labor hours | 16 hours total | — | | Labor rate | $35/hour billed | — | | Labor cost | 16 × $35 | $560 | | Supplies/travel | — | $100 | | **Subtotal** | | **$660** | | Profit margin | Add 25–40% | — | | **Bid** | | **≈ $825–$950** | --- ## Never Forget These Add-Ons Charge separately for: - Heavy debris removal - Interior windows - Appliance cleaning - Garage cleaning - Exterior areas - Extra visits - Rush scheduling --- ## Payment Rules **For larger jobs** (e.g., a $2,500 project): - Request 30–50% deposit - Balance due after completion **For contractors** — know your net terms *before* accepting the job: - Net 7 - Net 15 - Net 30 --- ## After Every Job Create a folder per customer, containing: - Contract - Estimate - Photos - Invoice - Payment - Review --- ## Your First 90-Day Victory List By day 90: - ☐ Insurance active - ☐ Vendor packet complete - ☐ 300+ contractor contacts - ☐ 10+ estimates sent - ☐ 5 completed jobs - ☐ 3 reviews - ☐ 1 recurring contractor relationship
