Business Discovery

What’s working — and what’s getting in the way?

A short discovery questionnaire to understand the systems, tools, and processes businesses rely on today — and the problems they wish someone would fix.
Question 1 of 911%
01 · Business

Tell us about the business.

We’re looking for patterns across different types of businesses.
02 · Current system

What systems do you rely on every day?

Think software, apps, spreadsheets, websites, POS systems, CRMs, scheduling tools, accounting tools, or custom systems.
03 · The good stuff

What do you LOVE about your current system?

What makes you say, “This is why we keep using it”?
04 · Friction

Where does the current system fall short?

Be specific. Small annoyances are useful because they often reveal bigger workflow problems.
05 · Wish list

If you could add anything, what would you want?

This is the most important question. Don’t worry about whether the feature exists yet.
06 · Manual work

What are you still doing manually?

Manual entry, copying information, texting customers, chasing payments, scheduling, reports — anything counts.
07 · Business impact

What does the problem cost you?

A rough answer is fine. We’re trying to understand impact, not audit your books.
08 · The one fix

If someone could fix ONE thing tomorrow, what would it be?

Forget everything else. What single improvement would make the biggest difference?
09 · Optional follow-up

Want us to follow up?

Leave your contact information only if you’re open to discussing the problem you described. You can submit anonymously.
