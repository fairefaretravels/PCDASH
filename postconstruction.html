1. Service Packages & Pricing Guide

Package 1: Basic Final Clean

Best for: Small renovations, light construction

Includes:

  • Construction dust removal
  • Vacuuming and mopping
  • Surface wipe-down
  • Bathroom cleaning
  • Kitchen cleaning
  • Trash bag removal (inside property only)
  • Final walkthrough touch-up
Starting price: $0.20–$0.35 per square foot

Package 2: Detailed Builder Final Clean

Best for: New builds and major remodels

Includes everything in Basic, plus:

  • Cabinet interior/exterior cleaning
  • Baseboards and trim detailing
  • Doors and frames
  • Fixtures polished
  • Window interior cleaning
  • Appliance cleaning
  • Detailed inspection checklist
Starting price: $0.35–$0.60 per square foot

Package 3: Premium Move-In Ready Clean

Best for: Luxury homes, real estate, high-end renovations

Includes:

  • Photo documentation
